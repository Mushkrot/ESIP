# Signal Desk visual system

Signal Desk is the ESIP interface theme. It is designed for dense operational work: quiet surfaces,
clear hierarchy, compact evidence, and restrained status colour.

## Identity

The ESIP mark is a four-channel signal grid. Its channels correspond to ticket evidence, operating
context, analytics, and knowledge meeting in one workspace.

- Primary mark: `public/esip-mark.svg`
- Application icon: `public/esip-icon-128.png`
- Product name: ESIP
- Expanded name: Enterprise Support Intelligence Platform

## System values

- Deep petrol `#103B37` anchors navigation and identity.
- Warm canvas `#ECEAE2` separates the workspace from browser chrome.
- Signal teal `#2F8F83` marks selection, focus, and primary actions.
- Copper `#C27643` is reserved for secondary signals.
- IBM Plex Sans is the primary face; Avenir Next and platform sans-serif fonts are fallbacks.
- IBM Plex Mono is used for identifiers and machine state.
- Spacing follows a 4 px base with an `8 / 12 / 16 / 24` working rhythm.
- Controls and panels use 6 to 10 px radii. Pills are limited to compact status labels.
- Motion lasts 160 ms and uses small opacity or position changes. Reduced-motion preferences are
  honored.

Semantic success, warning, and failure colours keep their conventional meaning. The navigation plane
stays petrol in both themes so the product remains recognizable without relying on a wordmark.
